Privacy policy

Your data is associated with your signed-in Firebase account. Access depends on the application's Firebase security rules.

Do not store passwords, payment card details, or other highly sensitive information in notes. Settings are stored in your account so they can synchronize across devices.

When you send feedback, the information you enter is sent to the configured feedback service.

Terms of use

Use this application lawfully and keep your account credentials secure.

Cloud synchronization requires an internet connection, a valid Firebase configuration, and suitable database rules. Keep independent backups of important information.

Features may depend on the configuration and deployment of the application.
